Old system → New website converter

Open your old db.json in Notepad, copy ALL of it, paste below, click Convert. Then paste the produced SQL into Supabase → SQL Editor → Run. It carries over: your dashboards (content), scenes, rotation schedule, and crop/fit settings. Local-only items (logo slices, uploaded images, colors) are skipped — re-upload the logo with the console's 21-slice tool.
